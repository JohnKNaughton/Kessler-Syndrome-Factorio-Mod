# Kessler Syndrome: Scrapsteroids Above Fulgora

A Factorio Space Age mod. Fulgora's orbit is choked with centuries of accumulated industrial debris — Scrapsteroids litter the transit lanes and pack the orbital belt in overwhelming numbers. Mine them for scrap, or run advanced processing to extract depleted uranium fuel cells and nuclear fuel from the radioactive wreckage.

## What it adds

**Two asteroid entities**
- **Scrapsteroid** (small) — glows faintly green, found on transit routes approaching Fulgora
- **Medium Scrapsteroid** — larger variant found only in Fulgora's orbit, yields more chunks per hit

**One chunk item**
- **Scrapsteroid Chunk** — subtle green-tinted metallic icon

**Two crusher recipes**

| Recipe | Unlock | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Scrapsteroid Processing | From start | 5 scrap |
| Geiger Scrapsteroid Processing | Nuclear Power research | 3 scrap + 10% depleted uranium fuel cell + 0.5% nuclear fuel |

## Spawning

**Transit routes** (Nauvis, Vulcanus, Gleba ↔ Fulgora): Nothing until within 40% of the distance to Fulgora, then building to a dense cluster at arrival. Direction-aware — the sparse zone always faces the non-Fulgora end.

**Fulgora orbit**: 10× the combined probability of all other asteroid types. Overwhelmingly scrap. Medium Scrapsteroids also appear at the same rate as medium metallic asteroids.

## Settings

**Scrapsteroid Spawn Rate Multiplier** (startup setting, default 1.0, range 0.1–10.0) — scales all spawn rates up or down.

## Requirements

- Factorio 2.0
- Space Age DLC
